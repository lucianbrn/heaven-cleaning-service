Questions ———

Everything clear, up front.

How much does a clean cost?

Prices are published above: Standard from $120, Deep from $180, Move-In/Out from $250, by home size. Ellie confirms your final price personally before the visit — no surprises.

What is the difference between the three cleans?

The Standard maintains: dusting, counters and mirrors, floors, a kitchen wipe-down and a light bathroom clean. The Deep adds baseboards, door frames, polished fixtures, a full bathroom clean and a kitchen deep clean. Move-In/Out gets inside cabinets, drawers, the oven and the fridge — made for handovers.

Which clean is best for a first visit?

Most homes start with a Deep Clean — it brings everything up to the Heaven level — then keep the calm with a Standard every week or two.

Are the insides of the oven and fridge included?

In a Move-In/Out Clean, yes. For any other clean you can add them: fridge +$35, oven +$30.

Which areas do you serve?

Northern Virginia: Falls Church, McLean, Vienna, Fairfax, Arlington, Alexandria, Tysons, Annandale, Dunn Loring and Merrifield. If your town is not listed, ask — Ellie always tries to help a neighbor.

Send the booking form below. Ellie reviews it and confirms personally with your final price and time. We speak English and Spanish — book whichever way is easier for you.
